HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
1. Edit text in the [EDIT] / SUGGESTED fields. Leave CURRENT blocks as reference
   until the team updates the React pages.
2. Keep each page light: one clear job per section (headline + short support line).
   Do not pack the first viewport with stats, schedules, or secondary promos.
3. SEO: every route needs a UNIQUE meta title and meta description.
   Aim ~50–60 characters for titles; ~150–160 characters for descriptions.
4. Do NOT invent legal claims, registration numbers, tax benefits, or impact
   figures that are not already on the site or confirmed by Shikshantar.
5. Mark anything speculative clearly as SUGGESTED. Verified site facts stay under
   CURRENT.
6. After editing here, hand the revised lines to a developer to update the UI
   (or paste into the matching section in Index.tsx / About.tsx / components).
